BranchOut

Often many programmers who are interested in open source only know what it means but lack an understanding of how to participate. BranchOut seeks to fill that gap by bringing new or experienced programmers to open source projects that need their help. Through its main pages people can filter projects they are interested in and think they are a good fit for by skill level, skill set, and proficiency. BranchOut also seeks to get help to open source projects that need it as people can invite others to collaborate with them on their projects to keep them maintained or to get them off the ground.
